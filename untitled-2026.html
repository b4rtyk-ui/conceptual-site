untitled(2026)


type: video

year: 2026


conceptual information:

Inspired by Japanese Mingei philosophy and by an interest in overlooked human gestures, this video uses slowed and manipulated footage from a live show recorded on a low-quality 2008 iPod (the same exact one it is displayed on). The original concert sound was removed and replaced with an artificial sound environment, shifting the work away from performance and toward a more reflective study of movement, atmosphere and collective presence. Human figures appear here more directly than in the rest of the exhibition, but they are still treated as traces within a larger field of observation. Within the exhibition, the piece broadens the show’s focus from the marks people leave on objects to the subtle ways human presence itself can be framed, edited and made strange through systems of representation.
