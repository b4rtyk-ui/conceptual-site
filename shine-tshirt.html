shine t-shirt


type: garment

year: 2025


conceptual information:

Inspired by the sharp sparkle of snow under light, this work transforms a blank 1990s T-shirt into a reactive surface that changes depending on how it is viewed. Using varnish, pearlescent powder and white paint, a finish has been crafted. It only fully activates under direct light, so the piece reveals itself through movement, angle and close attention. Within the exhibition, the work makes the viewer an active participant, because it can only be fully understood through inspection, reflection and proximity.
