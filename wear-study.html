wear study


type: digital collage

year: 2025


conceptual information:

Inspired by close observation and by the idea of everyday objects as records of use, this photographic grid documents repeated areas of wear on 9 different pairs of worn blue jeans. Each image of the exact same spot on different pairs was taken with the same lighting, framing and distance so that the viewer focuses on the differences in erosion, friction, and fading rather than on composition. The work turns ordinary signs of use into something worth studying carefully. Within the exhibition, it works as a system of comparison, showing how time and repeated human movement leave visible marks on similar objects.
