vermont t-shirt


type: garment

year: 2025


conceptual information:

Inspired by the 1944 jazz song Moonlight in Vermont, this work connects place, memory and material transformation through a worn white T-shirt from the early 2000s. The large central print combines a digitally rusted maple leaf, small text and botanical line drawings, turning the shirt into a surface where geography, memory and time are layered together. Within the exhibition, the piece expands the idea of human trace beyond physical wear alone, showing how a garment can also carry cultural memory, atmosphere and place.
