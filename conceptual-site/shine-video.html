shine (video)


type: video

year: 2025


conceptual information:

Inspired by early 2000s holiday media aesthetics and by the close examination of material surfaces, this video presents recorded footage of the altered reflective T-shirt against a kitschy digital gradient background. The slowed clips, artificial festive colors and layered ambient sound create a strange contrast between cheerful media nostalgia and the more investigative act of looking closely at an object. By placing the tactile shirt surface inside a highly constructed digital space, the work explores how media framing changes the emotional meaning of material things. Within the exhibition, the video extends the T-shirt piece into moving image, showing how surfaces can be reinterpreted and transformed through editing, sound and digital presentation.
