bruise jeans


type: garment

year: 2025


conceptual information:

Inspired by the way bruises hold evidence of force beneath the skin, this piece treats denim as a surface that can absorb pressure, friction and time in a similar way. The jeans were worn, soaked, bent, sanded and bleached through a slow and controlled process so that the marks would emerge through bodily action rather than look factory-made. Distressed leather patches at the knees refer to both vulnerability and reinforcement, since knees are one of the body’s main points of pressure and contact. Within the exhibition, the work introduces the idea that clothing can function as a record of human impact, holding traces of movement, wear and damage.
