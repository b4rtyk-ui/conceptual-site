WORK TITLE


type: assemblage

year: 2025


conceptual information:

Inspired by museum conservation, observation, and material honesty, this piece presents a sequence of fabric, paper, and leather fragments that have been lightly treated to reveal their surfaces more clearly. Rather than trying to make the materials look dramatic, the work allows subtle staining, dyeing, and repetition to make texture, weight, and age easier to notice. The arrangement is highly controlled, with each fragment separated by measured gaps so that every material can be read both on its own and as part of a sequence. Within the exhibition, the work slows the viewer down and encourages careful looking, reinforcing the show’s interest in trace, patina, and the quiet evidence held by ordinary materials.
