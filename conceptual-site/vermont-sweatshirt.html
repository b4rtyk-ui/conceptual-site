Vermont Sweatshirt


type: garment

year: 2025


conceptual information:

Working as a quieter companion to the Vermont T-shirt, this sweatshirt approaches memory as something partial, soft and difficult to fully recover. Printed cotton patches were inserted into the garment so the sweatshirt becomes a container for fragments of image, text and place. One patch holds a faded aerial fragment of Montpelier, Vermont, while another carries the place name on its own, leaving the location incomplete and partly obscured. Within the exhibition, the work functions as a more residual and intimate version of the Vermont idea, focusing on what remains after memory becomes worn, fragmented and less direct.
