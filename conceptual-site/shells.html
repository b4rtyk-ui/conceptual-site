shells


type: object

year: 2025


conceptual information:

Inspired by boxing as a controlled outlet for human aggression, this work transforms a pair of nearly 30-year-old gloves into a hollow sculptural object. The inner padding was removed so the gloves could no longer protect or perform their original function, leaving only the worn outer skin of the object behind. Metal studs taken from a PRL-era belt were added only to the areas that would normally receive impact, so the intervention follows function rather than decoration. Within the exhibition, the piece brings in the idea of violence, protection, and time, showing how an object can preserve human action even after it stops being useful.
